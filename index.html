About us

Thidal Sports is a floodlit artificial-turf ground in Palavedu, Avadi, built for football and cricket. The pitch is enclosed with high safety netting and lit end to end, so games run comfortably well past sunset.

  • SportsFootball & cricket
  • SurfaceArtificial turf, fully netted
  • LightingLED floodlights for night play

Book online through TurfTown, or call us to reserve a slot.
